Turns every glyph into a vector outline. The pages look the same and stay sharp at any zoom, but the text can no longer be selected, copied or searched.

Download traced PDF
Good to know
  • Your file never leaves this device. Ghostscript runs in your browser as WebAssembly.
  • The first run downloads about 15 MB. After that, the browser loads it from its cache.
  • The output is usually somewhat larger than the input.
  • Form fields, annotations and Type 3 fonts may not survive the conversion. Check the result.
  • This only deters copying: screenshots and OCR still recover the text. Search and screen-reader access are lost by design.
